## 🧭 About Me

<img align="right" alt="Coding" width="380" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abhisheknaiidu/abhisheknaiidu/master/code.gif" />

I'm a software engineer and aspiring **AI/ML Engineer** based in **Kampala, Uganda 🇺🇬**, on a focused journey toward becoming a world-class **AI Engineer, Machine Learning Engineer, and Data Analyst**. I love the full arc of data work — from messy raw inputs, through clean pipelines and well-trained models, to production systems people actually use.

- 🎯 **My Mission:** Grow into a complete AI/ML Engineer & Data Analyst who ships intelligent, real-world systems.
- 🔭 **Currently Building:** End-to-end ML workflows, analytics dashboards, and AI-powered business automation.
- 🌱 **Sharpening Right Now:** Advanced **Python** & **SQL** — the bedrock of data engineering and MLOps.
- ⚙️ **Exploring Next:** MLOps, CI/CD, cloud ML deployment, and containerized model serving.
- 💬 **Ask Me About:** Machine Learning, Data Analysis, SQL, Python, Backend APIs, and Full-Stack systems.
- 🏢 **Founder @** [Persmon Technologies](https://persmontechnologies.com) — turning data into decisions for businesses.
- 🎹 **Off the Keyboard:** Composing piano melodies and creating digital art.

> *"Data is the new soil — my goal is to grow intelligent systems in it."*
